FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How does ECHO-ASSIST connect to the smart cane?

The system connects via Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE 5.0) to the onboard ESP32 controller. Sensor packets (ultrasonic, ToF laser, battery, accelerometer) are transmitted with sub-50ms latency.

Can the platform function offline or with poor connectivity?

Yes. The smart cane maintains local haptic vibration and proximity buzzer alerts autonomously on the ESP32 hardware, even when internet connection drops.

What languages are supported for voice assistance?

ECHO-ASSIST AI supports native natural language TTS and voice guidance in English, Hindi, and Telugu, with additional regional languages continuously in development.

How does the emergency fall detection system work?

The 6-axis IMU accelerometer identifies sudden freefall and impact dynamics. A configurable countdown (default 30s) allows the user to cancel false alarms before automatic SMS and phone calls dispatch to registered guardians.

Is patient and location data secure?

Yes. All communication uses TLS 1.3 encryption, database credentials and passwords are salt-hashed, and user sessions adhere to strict account-level scoping.
